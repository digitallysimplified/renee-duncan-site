AboutRenee

Dr. Renee Duncan is an educator, researcher and education technology specialist based in Brisbane, Queensland. After more than a decade working in New York, she has returned home to Australia, bringing international experience in teaching, professional learning and digital innovation.

Her work focuses on helping educators and organisations use technology with confidence, from AI and Google Workspace to digital design, through practical, research-informed training and support.

  • Ed.D. Columbia University, USA
  • M.A. New York University, USA
  • B.A. / Grad Dip Griffith University, Australia
