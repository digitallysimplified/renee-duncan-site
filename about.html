AboutRenee

Dr. Renee Duncan is an educator, researcher and learning designer based in Brisbane, Queensland. For ten years she was Band Director and Music Teacher at Columbia Grammar and Preparatory School in New York, where she designed project-based curriculum and authentic assessment for face-to-face, blended and online learning, created digital resources and instructional videos, and led professional development for colleagues on Google Workspace and digital tools.

As Digital Media Manager for the Music Educators Association of New York City, she led a full website migration and built an e-commerce platform that grew website traffic by 277% and online sales by 167% over three years. She also reviews research for the American Educational Research Association in instructional technology, curriculum and instruction, and assessment.

Renee began her career teaching English and Music with the Queensland Department of Education and has now returned home to Queensland.
