AboutRenee

With over a decade of teaching and research experience across Australia and the United States, Dr. Renee Duncan specialises in curriculum design, educational technology and online learning. She holds an Ed.D. and M.Ed. from Teachers College, Columbia University, an M.A. from New York University, a G.Dip. from Griffith University, and a B.Mus. from the Queensland Conservatorium of Music.

Dr. Duncan has published in Journal of General Music Education and Teaching Music, and has presented at state, national and international conferences for organisations including the International Society for Music Education and the National Association for Music Education.

Renee has worked with educators, non-profits and small businesses to streamline their online courses and digital workflows, and to learn new technology tools that reduce stress and technology overwhelm.
